Hi, I'm Kateryna and

Design isn't just

about aesthetics. It's about

decisions that make sense.

Design at the intersection of logic, aesthetics, and product thinking.

My approach is both analytical and creative.

I research before I design, test before I deliver. I think of a product as a system — every screen has a reason to be exactly as it is.

A fine arts education gave me a different perspective — hard to explain, but it shows up in the work. Artistic thinking helps me see possibilities; research and analysis turn them into product decisions that hold up.

I care about design that shapes how people behave and decide. Not just how a product looks — but how well it actually helps them get things done.

How I approach my work

01

Understand before designing.

Most design mistakes start with solving the wrong problem. I begin with research — to make sure I'm solving the right thing, not just drawing screens.

02

Think in systems, not pages.

Every screen is part of a larger logic. I design with before and after in mind. That's what makes a product feel consistent and intentional.

03

Test assumptions early.

Ideas are cheap. Feedback is what makes them valuable. I test quickly, honestly, and without attachment to any one solution.

04

Details are the difference.

The gap between good and great design lives in small things. Spacing, states, micro-interactions — I think about these even when nobody notices right away.
